The honest part

What These Books Are, and What They Are Not

Written for the later years. These books are only about older cats and dogs: comfort, daily rhythm and a good day. They do not cover puppies, kittens, training plans or choosing a pet.

Vet first, every time. Both books are general care information. They never diagnose, never name a medicine or a dose, and always send a change in eating, drinking, toileting, sleep, mood or movement to your vet.

No promised years. Nobody can promise a lifespan. Good care and comfort give an older pet the best chance of good days, and that is the aim of every chapter.

No brand names, no shopping spree. Beds, ramps, bowls and harnesses are described by type, and most of the book uses what you already have at home.

Kind to you too. There is a chapter for the person who looks after the pet, and honest, gentle chapters on hard days, the decision, and the grief that follows.

Made to print. Ten cards in each book, from the Good-Day Rhythm Planner to the Vet Now card and the Sitter's Care Sheet, are set one to a page for plain paper.
