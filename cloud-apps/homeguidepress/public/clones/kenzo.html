Sound familiar?

You still want to go out. You just want to feel steady.

Maybe the walk home after dark feels longer than it used to. Maybe a quiet parking lot makes you hold your keys a little tighter. Or you simply move more slowly now, and you notice who is around you.

None of that means you should stay home. Calm and Ready is about going out with your eyes up, your hands free and a plan in your head, so worry has less room.

It will not turn you into a fighter, and it does not try to. It helps you stay calm, notice early and step away.

The best trouble is the trouble you walk away from.

The idea at the heart of the guide.
