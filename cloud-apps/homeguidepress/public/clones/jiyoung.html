Know Your Skin and Patch Test First

A short, quiet check before a new cleanser, cream, sunscreen or makeup reaches your face.

the one new productclean, dry handsa small notebookdaylightlukewarm water
1
Wash and dry your hands. Pick just one new product.
2
Choose a small, clean spot: the inner forearm or behind the ear.
3
Apply a little, as the maker says. More does not help.
4
Wait as long as the label asks. Without a note, a day or two is a careful choice.
5
Look at the spot in daylight each day for redness, bumps or swelling.
6
Anything there? Stop, rinse with lukewarm water and set it aside.
7
Still calm? Try a small patch of your face, like the jawline, for a few days.
✓
Write one line in your skin diary, then wait before the next new thing.

Take care: stop at once if you feel stinging or itching, or see redness. If a rash spreads, swells or hurts, see a doctor. The guide marks steps like this with a Take Care box.

06Small tips inside 작은 팁

A few of the notes you will find in the margins

Too much at once? Begin with a single product and a single minute. Add the next step only when the first one feels like a habit.

Start smallPart 1

Keep your sunscreen where you pass every morning, by the toothbrush or the kettle. The step you can see is the step you do.

Sun carePart 4

After you cleanse, put the kettle on, then come back for your moisturizer. That short pause helps layers stay put.

LayersPart 2

Check your base at a window with a hand mirror held at chest height. Daylight shows what a lamp hides.

Light makeupPart 3

Color too strong? Do not start over. Press with a clean tissue or fingertip and it softens.

Cheeks and lipsPart 3

Hand creams and balms count as new products too. Test each one, one at a time.

HandsPart 2

Tidy brows and nails once, calmly, at a bright window. Then you are done for the week.
