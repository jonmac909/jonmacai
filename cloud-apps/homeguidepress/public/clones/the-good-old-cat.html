The honest part

What This Book Is, and What It Is Not

Written for the later years. This book is only about older cats: comfort, daily rhythm and a good day. It does not cover kittens, training or choosing a cat.

Vet first, every time. This is general care information. It never diagnoses, never names a medicine or a dose, and always sends a change in eating, drinking, toileting, sleep, mood or movement to your vet.

No promised years. Nobody can promise a lifespan. Good care and comfort give an older cat the best chance of good days, and that is the aim of every chapter.

No brand names, no shopping spree. Beds, steps, bowls and litter boxes are described by type, and most of the book uses what you already have at home.

Kind to you too. There is a chapter for the person who looks after the cat, and honest, gentle chapters on hard days, the decision, and the grief that follows.

Made to print. Ten cards, from the Good-Day Rhythm Planner to the Vet Now card and the Sitter's Care Sheet, are set one to a page for plain paper.
