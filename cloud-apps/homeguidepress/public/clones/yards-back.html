Is it for you?

This is for you if…

  • You have noticed the ball does not go as far as it used to, and swinging harder only makes it worse.
  • You miss the center of the face more often than you would like.
  • Long irons feel like hard work and you would like an easier club for long shots.
  • You want gentle movement and simple swing basics that respect the body you have today. No gym, no swing overhaul.

It is not for you if you want a quick fix or a promise of a number of yards. This book does not make one, and it asks you to check with your doctor or physiotherapist before you start.

Meet the author

Hi, I'm Gail

I wrote Yards Back for golfers over 55 who still love the game and want it to feel easier.

The book is general golf and fitness information in plain words: gentle movement, simple swing basics, equipment explained by type, practice plans and smart course management. Questions

Before you
decide

Do I need to be strong or flexible?

No. Every movement is gentle, has an easier version and comes with a stop line. The book asks you to see a doctor or physiotherapist before you start any new movement, and to follow their advice over the book.

Will it add yards?

The book does not promise any number of yards, and nobody honestly can. It focuses on solid contact, a free turn, a tempo you can keep and equipment that suits you, which many golfers find makes the game feel easier. See how it feels, and write it down.

Do I need new clubs?

No. The equipment part explains things by type, such as loft, shaft weight and grip size, and tells you what to ask a qualified club fitter. It is there to help you decide, not to sell you anything.

Is it medical advice?

No. It is general golf and fitness information. See a doctor or physiotherapist before you start any new movement or exercise, especially if you have had surgery or have a health condition, and stop if anything hurts.
