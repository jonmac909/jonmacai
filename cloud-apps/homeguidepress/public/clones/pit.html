Will training make him less watchful?

No. Most Pitbulls already care deeply about their family and their front door. You don't take that away. You give it an off switch.

The Alert-and-Settle routine teaches him to give one soft bark or stand at the door to tell you, then go to his mat when you say the word. A dog who alerts and then listens is a good watch dog. A dog who can't stop is a worry.

The book does not teach guard, bite or protection-sport training, and you shouldn't try that at home.

A health condition: the care chapters tell you plainly when to see your vet first.

New to dog training: yes. Every day has what you need, a timed plan, the steps, what to expect and one common slip with its fix.

Not a fit if your dog has bitten someone or shows real aggression. Work with a vet and a qualified behaviour professional first. The book says the same.
