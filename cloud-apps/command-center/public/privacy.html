Privacy policy

This covers Jon Mac Track, the private dashboard at jonmac.ai/dash that Jon Mac uses to run his own business. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads from Google

When Jon connects his own Google account, the dashboard reads his calendar events (title, start and end time) and, for his YouTube channels, channel and video statistics. It does not read email, contacts or files.

How it is used and kept

The data is shown only to Jon on his signed-in dashboard. It is stored in the dashboard's own database on Cloudflare, refreshed every few minutes, and never sold, shared or used for advertising. Google sign-in tokens are kept server side.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions. Stored calendar data is replaced on the next refresh and removed on request.
