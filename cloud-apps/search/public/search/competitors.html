Each rival

Arcads (home, terms, credits, refund)

  • Offer: 3-day trial with card, then Starter bills automatically. Refund only if no credits were used. No public pricing page.
  • Price: Starter about $110 a month ($77 promo), 8,000 credits, which is 10 one-actor videos of 60 s or less. About $11 per ad; a 15 s ad is billed as 60 s.
  • H1: "Create winning ads with AI". Hooks: "1,000+ AI actors", "Ready in 2.5 Minutes", "Save 50% vs Trad UGC". Bids on 10 rival names and on AI model names.
  • Proof: "used by millions", $16M seed, creator endorsers.
  • Not saying: prices are hidden; credits expire monthly; another client may get a "similar or identical" video with the same actor; your scripts train their AI; cloning needs an uploaded MP4, not a link.

Creatify (home, pricing, no refunds)

  • Offer: free plan, 10 credits a month (about 2 watermarked ads). No refunds.
  • Price: Starter $39 (100 credits), Pro $99 (300). Ad Clone is Pro only. Credits last 2 months. About $1.95 per ad (inferred).
  • H1: "AI Ads that win." Hooks: "Better Than Arcads – 75% Cheaper", "Clone Winning Ads with AI", "Steal Your Competitors Viral Ads With AI", "Paste a product URL. Get 10 video ads".
  • Proof: "18000+ Brands and Agencies", "15M+ Ads created", G2 4.8, $24M raised.
  • Not saying: no credits-per-video table; credits expire; Ad Clone locked to $99; shared stock avatars; every re-run costs credits.

MakeUGC (pricing, refund, Trustpilot)

  • Offer: "Start for $1", a 72-hour trial that renews at full price. Refund only if credits were not "substantially consumed".
  • Price: Startup $59 a month ($29 yearly), 500 credits = 5 videos. About $11.80 per ad.
  • H1: "The fastest way to create AI videos". Hooks: "Create Your 1st AI Ad for $1", "Don't pay $150 per video". Bids on "Arcads" and "Creatify".
  • Not saying: per-video cost only in API docs; credits expire; Trustpilot rating suspended after fake reviews were removed; charges after cancelling.

HeyGen (UGC page, pricing)

  • Offer: free plan, 3 videos a month with watermark. Creator $29 a month, 600 credits; credits per minute not published.
  • UGC page: "UGC Video Ads That Convert Without Hiring Creators". Its Google ads are mostly "Free AI Video Generator"; 2 of 125 sampled mention UGC.
  • Not saying: the UGC page says "unlimited videos" while plans are credit-capped; 1,100+ shared avatars; no ad cloning.

Higgsfield (help, Trustpilot)

  • Offer: no trial; refund within 7 days only if no credits were used.
  • Price: Plus $59 a month, 1,200 credits; a 15 s ad clip is 89.2 credits. About $8.77 per 30 s ad.
  • Hooks: "Create ads, product shots and UGC with 1,500+ templates", "From product link to video ad in 2 minutes". On Meta, rivals attack it: "I cancelled Higgsfield… Pennies per generation instead of $99 a month".
  • Not saying: credits expire; 15 s cap per generation; results are "regenerated, not edited"; trains on your content.

Topview (pricing, Arcads alternative page)

  • Offer: free trial, first month $9.50. Pro $29, Ultra $150. About $9 per 30 s ad on Pro.
  • Hooks: a price war on AI models ("Cheapest Seedance 2.5"), "Failed Task? Credits Back", "No credits expire" (its own pricing FAQ says they reset).
  • Not saying: credit costs can change "without prior notice"; "unlimited" means a slow queue.

invideo (UGC page, pricing)

  • Starter $20, 400 credits, about 2 ads a month. Its UGC page offers "approve every shot as a still", the only rival with a still-frame check, and it is not in their ads.

Tagshop (ad clone, terms)

  • Starter $29, 5 videos. "ALL FEES… NON-REFUNDABLE". Its Ad Clone (paste a Meta or TikTok link, copy hook, structure and timing) is the closest feature to Viral View. Almost no Google ads in the US since Aug 2.
  • Not saying: its comparison pages promise credits that don't expire; its terms say the opposite.

Pamba (ai-ugc, vs Arcads)

  • Free plan with 300 credits; Hobby $16. Built for organic posting, not paid ads. Clones from a TikTok link. About $3.20 per video on its own pricing ("from about $1" in its copy).

Captions / Mirage (credits)

  • Max $24.99, 500 credits. An editor first; no ad-structure cloning. Scripts capped at 30 s.

Icon (home, compare)

  • Now a human-creator agency: 6 filmed ads for $1,000 a month, 12 to 16 days. Its affiliate script tells partners to say "AI UGC is illegal as of June 9, 2026". That is false: New York's law requires disclosure of AI performers in ads; it is not a ban (Cooley). Expect "AI UGC failed" messages aimed at our buyers.

Billo (refund, AI FAQ)

  • Human creators from $99 a video, $89 editing; about $188 for an edited 15 s video, 7 to 12 business days after you ship the product. Ads: "Clear Pricing, No Surprises", "Videos From Just $99". Its own blog concedes AI costs "$2 to $10 per AI-generated asset versus $150 to $212" (blog).

Insense (pricing, refund)

  • "Try it for free" is a $650 trial month that becomes a $1,500 quarterly plan, plus creator fees from $100 and a 10% fee. About $250 per asset. Ads: "Andromeda Rewards Diversity", "270 Creatives Every Month".

Zeely (home): 700–800 ads aimed at small businesses, "ready-to-run video ad in under 60 seconds", $29.95 to $79.95 a month in credits.

Who attacks whom (competitor keyword targets)

  • Arcads is targeted by Creatify, MakeUGC, Topview, Tagshop and Pamba.
  • Higgsfield by Arcads, Creatify, MakeUGC, Topview, Tagshop, Pamba and creators on Meta.
  • Creatify by Arcads, MakeUGC, Topview, Tagshop and Pamba.
  • HeyGen by Arcads, Topview, Tagshop and Pamba.
  • MakeUGC by Topview, Tagshop and third-party review sites.
  • Billo by Creatify, Icon and Insense.

Topview, Tagshop and Pamba run full "alternative" and "vs" page sets. Third parties (wireflow, eesel, G2, superscale) also rank for "Arcads pricing" and "MakeUGC alternatives".
