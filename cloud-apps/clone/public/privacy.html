Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This page explains what is collected when you visit this site or buy the training.

What we collect

  • Purchase details — name, email and billing information, collected and stored by our payment processor, Commas. We receive your name, email and what you bought. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Survey answers — if you fill in the pre-workshop form, we receive what you type, including your name, email, phone number and answers about your business.
  • Whop advertising measurement — the Whop pixel records page views, opt-ins and checkout clicks across the funnel. Its _wuid cookie identifies an anonymous browser. We send verified paid transactions, email and campaign identifiers to Whop through its server events API to measure advertising and avoid duplicate conversions.
  • Analytics and advertising — if enabled, Meta Pixel and Google Analytics record page views, scroll depth, video plays and checkout clicks. Campaign parameters such as utm_source, fbclid and gclid are stored in your browser for the session and passed to checkout so a purchase can be attributed to the ad that produced it.

What we do with it

If you request text reminders, we record your phone number, your choice, the time you opted in, and the version of the consent disclosure. Text reminders are optional and consent is not required to purchase. Before reminders are activated, you will need to confirm your number. You can withdraw consent by replying STOP or contacting jon@thejonmac.com. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties for their marketing. A future messaging provider may process them only to deliver the reminders you request.

Our first-party session scorecard counts lander visits and verified payments and refunds. Its event records contain no email address, phone number or IP address. Advertising and attendance figures remain unavailable until their data sources are connected.

We use it to deliver the training, answer support requests, measure which advertising works, and tailor the session to the people attending. Survey answers are read by Jon Mac only.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, rent it, or pass it to unrelated third parties.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Commas — payments and receipts
  • Cloudflare — website delivery
  • Zoom — live workshop delivery
  • Whop — advertising measurement and conversion attribution
  • Meta and Google — advertising measurement, where enabled

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email. You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing jon@thejonmac.com. Browser-level ad tracking can be limited through your browser settings or your Meta and Google ad preferences.
